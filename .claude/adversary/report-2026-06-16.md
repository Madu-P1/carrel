### What the wins confirm
- The pass fires on every still-greening present type — governing_law, percent, and
  polarity (A4b) — and downgrades verified → unknown only when the wrapped quote is
  absent from the present's own clause.
- It does **not** over-refuse: verbatim wrapped quotes stay verified across CRLF,
  curly quotes, and the accepted leading-letter case flip (B1–B4).
- Pooling precision is real (D1): the check uses only the present-producing clause,
  so a quote that lives in an unrelated sibling clause cannot launder the present —
  and the engine refuses rather than guessing cross-clause attribution.
- The multi-quote quote-present exemption I worried could be a hole (E1/E2) is safe:
  a sentence with any non-verbatim quote never reaches a quote-present; it refuses.
