## P0 false greens (stop-the-world)

Both are PERCENT single-value subject-binding: the source carries exactly one
percent value, the claim binds it to a different subject, and the engine returns
`present`.

1. claim `"France's allocation is 20%."` vs source `"The allocation to Italy is 20% of turnover."` → `present`
2. claim `"The discount for early payment is 5%."` vs source `"A late-payment penalty of 5% applies to overdue invoices."` → `present`

Money and duration do NOT crack on the identical shape: ADR-0013 scope-out makes a
matching value → `not_found` (could-not-check), never `present`. Percent is the
outlier — it still affirms on value-match without binding the subject, and the
shared-content-token topicality gate (`_clause_on_topic` / `_shares_topic`) is too
coarse to catch France ≠ Italy. This is the known RT1 P0
(memory `cachet-confession-ledger-harness`); the percent scope-out designed in
ADR-0013 / PR `carrel#181` is NOT on `main`.

Held-out fixtures: `.claude/adversary/fixtures/percent-subject-binding/{01,02}.json`.

## Fix direction (routed to Forge, gated)

Extend the ADR-0013 scope-out to the percent `present` path so a percent
value-match degrades to could-not-check unless the subject is bound, mirroring
money/duration. This is a TRUTH-SURFACE change and a documented design decision,
not a mechanical edit:
- It must reconcile the existing percent-present tests
  (`test_percent_present_with_hedge_detail`, `test_dual_notation_of_one_rate_is_present_not_a_refusal`),
  which assert single-subject percent-present and must be revisited under the scope-out.
- The durable resolution per ADR-0013 is AFM subject-labeler PROPOSES + det engine
  DISPOSES + verbatim post-check; the pure-det engine cannot reliably bind subjects.
- See PR `carrel#181` (draft) for the already-designed percent scope-out — prefer
  landing/reconciling it over a fresh divergent implementation.

## Addendum — labeled-path fuzz (council follow-up, flips verdict to BROAD)

The council's NARROW verdict hinged on the subject-LABELED percent green being sound.
A fuzz pass settled it:
- 924/924 wrong-subject pairs at matching value HELD (real proper nouns: France 20% vs Italy 20%), 84/84 legit same-subject greens preserved — the binder is robust on REAL entities.
- BUT 8/8 clear FALSE GREENS when a capitalized COMMON word follows the % ("20% Interest" vs "20% Interest-free"; "20% Effective" rate vs "Effective date"; "20% Common" vs "Common shares were cancelled"). The `_PERCENT_SUBJECT` regex grabs any capitalized token as the subject and is blind to negation/polarity/concept-mismatch.

VERDICT FLIP: NARROW -> BROAD. The deterministic engine must NOT affirm a bare percent via the regex binder (subject-less OR weak-labeled). Mirror money/duration: percent value-match -> could-not-check; affirm only via the AFM subject labeler (ADR-0013). PR #181 (subject-less scope-out, keeps the labeled green) is INSUFFICIENT and must be extended. Held-out fixtures: .claude/adversary/fixtures/percent-labeled-binding/cracks.json.
