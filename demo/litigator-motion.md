# Plaintiff's Motion for Summary Judgment (excerpt)

The Equal Protection Clause forbids state-sponsored segregation in public
education. Brown v. Board of Education, 347 U.S. 483 (1954). Separate educational
facilities are inherently unequal and have no place in a system of public
schooling.

Procedural due process further requires notice and a meaningful opportunity to
be heard before the deprivation of a protected interest. Marbury v. Carcosa,
999 U.S. 999 (2021).

Accordingly, Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court grant summary
judgment in Plaintiff's favor.
