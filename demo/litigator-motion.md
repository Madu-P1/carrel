# Plaintiff's Motion for Summary Judgment (excerpt)

As the Supreme Court held, "Separate educational facilities are inherently
unequal." Brown v. Board of Education, 347 U.S. 483 (1954).

The Court likewise observed that "separate facilities are inherently equal" in
the modern context, Brown v. Board of Education, 347 U.S. 483.

Procedural due process requires notice and a meaningful opportunity to be heard
before the deprivation of a protected interest. Marbury v. Carcosa, 999 U.S. 999
(2021).

Accordingly, Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court grant summary
judgment in Plaintiff's favor.
