**Section 12. Notice of claim.**

(a) Written notice of a premises-liability claim against a commercial property owner shall be served upon the owner not later than ninety days after the date of the incident giving rise to the claim.

(b) The notice shall state the date of the incident, its approximate location, and the general nature of the condition alleged to have caused the injury.

(c) Failure to serve timely notice bars the claim unless the claimant demonstrates that the owner had actual knowledge of the incident within the notice period.

(d) Service of notice under this section does not extend any otherwise applicable statute of limitations and does not itself constitute the commencement of an action.
