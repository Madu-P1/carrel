Cachet certificate verifier

Paste or drop a Cachet certificate. Its seal is checked here, in your browser, offline, with no backend and no trust required: the fingerprint is recomputed as SHA-256 over the certificate's canonical body (keys sorted, "," and ":" separators, UTF-8) and compared against the declared one. Nothing is sent anywhere.

…or drop a .json file anywhere on this page.
The verdict on the certificate appears here.
A certificate records what a Cachet verification could and could not prove: every claim carries verified, altered, or could not check, and refusals carry the same weight as confirmations. An intact seal proves the record is exactly as the kernel issued it; it does not make any claim true. This page is a single file with no dependencies and no network use. Save it, host it, or hand it to whoever needs to check a record you gave them.
