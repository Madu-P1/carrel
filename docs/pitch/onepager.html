The study workspace that turns your deadlines into a system. Drop in any file you study from. Carrel plans your week, runs the spaced review, and keeps every answer honest with citations you can click and verify.

Universal ingest: PDFs, slides, Word docs, Markdown, Excel, plain text, and audio/image roadmap. Deadline-anchored Plan view that schedules against your real calendar. Spaced-repetition review tied to what you actually owe on Friday. Grounded Ask where every claim cites the verbatim span.

Problem

Studying is eight apps and a calendar that lies.

  • No tool ties what you study tonight to what you owe Friday. Calendars are passive; study apps are deadline-blind.
  • Lectures are PDFs, slides are PPTX, notes are Markdown, syllabi are HTML. Each tool owns one format.
  • AI study tools fabricate. A student cannot ship a wrong answer to an exam.
Insight

The deadline is the unit of work.

  • Start with the exam, the paper, the deliverable. Everything else allocates against it.
  • Universal ingest (10+ formats today) so the format never blocks the workflow.
  • Spaced review and concept graph compound across the semester; switching costs the term.
  • Citation chip flies to the source span on click. Verification in one gesture.
