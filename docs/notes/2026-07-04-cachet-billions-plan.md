## 1. The verdict: what Cachet is at billions scale

Cachet is not a checking tool. It is the company that owns the **certificate grammar
for machine-made claims** — the demanded mark. The billion-dollar mechanism is not
"every user pays for verification"; it is "every liability-bearing claim an AI writes
is a claim a risk-bearer can demand a certificate for, and the certificate format they
demand by name is Cachet's."

Three facts make this honest rather than grandiose:

1. **The demand side of attestation history is small and institutional.** Audit, credit
   ratings, UL, title, SOC 2: none were built on universal customers. A handful of
   demanders (regulators, insurers, counterparties) conscripted an entire supply side.
   The council's "the customer is not universal" was never an argument against a
   universal-scale company; it described the shape of every one that exists.
2. **Bundling destroys the thing bundling is supposed to kill Cachet with.** Platforms
   can bundle verification but cannot bundle independence from themselves. Every
   bundled self-check that fails in court (the entire 2026 sanctions record, including
   filings prepared WITH CoCounsel/Westlaw Precision) raises the value of the unbundled
   seat. The bundling wave is the scandal supply that compels the independent attestor.
3. **The option is nearly free.** The maximal company and the narrow wedge company
   share an identical next-90-days build (parity test → kernel extraction → daemon →
   legal wedge). What differs is four cheap postural moves (§5, Phase 1). Until a
   compulsion trigger fires, they are the same company.

**The historian's one-line law, which governs everything below:** every attestation
business became big at the exact moment a risk-bearer who was NOT the professional
stopped accepting the professional's own word. That moment has not arrived for legal
AI — and the 2026 record is currently moving the OTHER way (courts and bars are
doubling down on lawyer SELF-attestation: Rule 11, ABA 512, Texas 705, the 40+
standing orders all say "certify that YOU checked"). Plan accordingly (§2, item 3).

## 2. What you cannot see from inside (ranked by how much it changes)

**1. Your default revenue model is the rating-agency conflict wearing a different
suit.** The firm pays Cachet to bless the firm's own output. That is issuer-pays —
the exact structure that turned Moody's/S&P from trusted intermediaries into the
villains of 2008, and it structurally undermines the one thing you sell:
independence. The historical fix (UL/IIHS): get the **risk-bearer** — the malpractice
carrier — to be the party who compels or credits, so the attested party's payment
buys the *check*, and the *demand* comes from someone with skin in the game. Organize
the company's demand side around the carrier, not the firm.

**2. You already shipped the billion-dollar half and file it under UI.** The
Certification Exhibit + seal + in-browser Seal Bench is the demandable artifact the
whole strategy hangs on, and it is treated as build-steps 6 and 8 of a frontend
rebuild. The mispriced asset is the *verifier* side of that pair: checking a
certificate is free, zero-COGS, account-less, and infinitely distributable. A free
public verifier (static page + CLI + Word button) puts the format in every
demander's hands and teaches every recipient that the format exists. No plan
document treats it as a distribution channel. It is the distribution channel.

**3. Today, Cachet is a self-attestation tool in a market being pushed toward
self-attestation — and that is fine, if you stop confusing the two businesses.**
Sold as legal pre-flight, Cachet helps the lawyer check their own work before they
sign (a real, fundable, paying business: it is how you earn the right to be the
mark). The compelled-independent-artifact business is a different business with a
different buyer, and on the 2026 evidence its trigger has not fired. Sell the first
honestly and at full price now; build the second's preconditions cheaply (§5); never
report revenue from the first as validation of the second.

**4. Two of your own documents dissolve the Windows/VDI objection and have never
been introduced to each other.** The validation round logs Windows/Citrix-VDI as the
Wedge-2 killer (Harvey: "the elegant macOS-native story is aimed at a population
that mostly is not in the regulated segments that need it"). But the ratified
packaging ruling makes zero-egress a PROCESS property, provable by socket ban +
packet capture — a property a signed Windows service inside a Citrix VM satisfies.
The macOS app has a platform problem; the daemon north star does not. Write the
connecting sentence; make "Windows daemon" a priced, gate-triggered line item
instead of a kill-log entry.

**5. You are watching for the compulsion trigger when you could be a cause of it —
but the historian corrects the aim.** A carrier premium credit is private ordering:
no court, no bar committee, founder-reachable. However, carriers are currently
EXCLUDING AI risk (Hamilton, Berkley, Verisk/ISO CG 40 47/48 effective Jan 2026),
not underwriting it — excluding is cheaper. So the near-term move is discovery, not
sales: put the pre-registered question to an underwriter ("would you price a credit
for certified filings, or just exclude?"). And watch the true leading indicator:
**the first coverage DENIAL on an AI-hallucination claim**, not the next sanction.
Sanctions have piled up without flipping the market; a denial forces firms to demand
affirmative terms, and affirmative terms are where a verification requirement
attaches.

**6. Kernel-licensing risks the title-insurance outcome.** In title insurance the
distribution channel (agents) captured ~80% of the premium; insurers competed for
the referrer, not the insured. If the buyer asks "where's the verification?" and the
answer is "it's built into Westlaw," Cachet captured nothing. Licensing into
Word/iManage is fine ONLY as distribution for the mark: the output of any embedded
kernel must be a Cachet-sealed certificate, named, verifiable on the free bench.
Value accrues to whoever owns the artifact demanded BY NAME — decide this explicitly
now, not by default later.

**7. RESOLVED same day (2026-07-04): the cheapest decisive experiment had in fact
already been run — and passed.** Council trigger #3's answer existed at
`evals/reports/kernel-residue-2026-07-02.md` (15/15 catch, clean floor) but was
never registered in any strategy doc, so two councils and one advocate argued a
question whose answer was on disk. It was reproduced on main and then attacked
with a fresh out-of-distribution rotation (26 attacks, 8 families): **honesty
floor held 26/26, zero false greens, zero false accusations; OOD catch 9/15 with
every miss an honest refusal.** The kernel-generality branch of trigger #3 fires
POSITIVE; Vulcan's extraction gate is cleared. See
`docs/notes/2026-07-04-trigger3-verdict-registered.md` and
`.claude/adversary/report-2026-07-04.md`. The residual lesson stands: a run but
unregistered experiment does not exist for decision-making.

**8. The certificate needs greens to be worth demanding.** The refusal-as-hero
instinct — your best asset — is over-rotated one notch past where your own ADRs put
the line. Provably-safe greens exist (ADR-0013's own consequences: governing-law,
polarity anchors). A demandable certificate on a mostly-correct document must read
as an instrument that CLEARS claims with refusals scoped, or it is "a filable
admission of incompleteness." Never-LIE-green is the invariant; never-SHOW-green is
presentation theology.

**9. You are running a standards play by accident — finish it on purpose.** ADR-0015
froze the 3-state + provenance wire schema forever, additive-only. That is the
opening move of a standard. Standards are won by publishing the schema, the
conformance suite, and reference verifiers early, so that when a compulsion event
arrives there is exactly one format for it to name. The engineering is done; the
strategic act (publish) costs a week and appears in no plan.

**10. The prosumer tier was executed with a mass-consumer argument.** Solo
practitioners, freelance tax preparers, expert witnesses, journalists with
corrections policies: damage that is attributable, personal, liability-bearing (the
council's own willingness-to-pay test) — but they buy like consumers: self-serve,
sub-$500/yr, no procurement. This is not the dead ambient companion; it is a
self-serve tier of the same daemon, and your JetBrains-style pricing posture already
assumes it.

## 3. The end-state product, curated

Everything extends the ratified architecture (kernel + daemon + frozen 3-state wire
contract + packs that structurally cannot pronounce green). No layer changes; each
extends.

### 3.1 `cachetd` — the attestation daemon
- One signed, zero-egress loopback process. macOS first; **Windows (incl. VDI
  images) as a gate-triggered second target** — that build is what makes the
  regulated segments addressable at all.
- `POST /verify {claim|document, sources[], pack} → {verdict: verified|altered|
  could_not_check, provenance[], certificate?}`. Frozen, additive-only.
- Enterprise fleet mode: MDM-deployable, org-signed policy for which packs, corpora,
  and evidence roots are trusted or policy-excluded.

### 3.2 The certificate — the actual product
- Portable signed JSON + rendered PDF. Carries hashes and verdicts, **never document
  content** — the certificate travels where the document cannot; the zero-egress
  moat extends to the artifact itself.
- **Anyone-can-verify, free forever, no account:** public Seal Bench page + CLI +
  Word button. The signer pays; the demander never does (the notarization economics).
- **Demand-side artifacts Cachet drafts for its demanders:** a model standing-order
  clause, a model outside-counsel-guidelines paragraph, a model carrier-endorsement
  rider — each naming the certificate schema. Be the reference text the compeller
  copy-pastes.

### 3.3 Surfaces (all thin adapters over the daemon)
- The app (lectern / verdict / exhibit / vault / shelf) — the craft surface, being
  rebuilt to the design handoff; the verdict reveal is the money moment.
- Word/Outlook add-in; iManage/Relativity embeds (licensing channel, mark-preserving
  per §2.6).
- CI/API gate: a pipeline step that refuses AI-drafted claims (compliance text,
  filings, changelogs) without a green-or-refused certificate. Same daemon, headless.
- **Demander-side batch verifier** (the BriefCatch lesson inverted): a free bulk
  Seal Bench for clerks, carriers, audit committees. Put the checker in the
  compeller's hands.

### 3.4 Domain packs (each gated on its own wedge economics)
Legal (shipped wedge) → tax (Circular 230 "substantial authority" = citation
grounding; kernel changes ~nothing) → audit/financial reporting (PCAOB AS 1105) →
then, only as evidence permits: financial IR, journalism (quotes vs transcripts),
insurance claims (claims vs policy text), procurement (bids vs RFP). Clinical last.
Third-party packs enter only through the conformance program; only the kernel
pronounces.

### 3.5 The standards & data moat
- **Publish the conformance corpus as the public benchmark.** Refusal-honesty is the
  one dimension where Cachet scores 9 and every incumbent 1–5; whoever publishes the
  benchmark frames the category. Publish the 11-metric rubric with its honesty
  caveats intact — that honesty IS the brand.
- **"Cachet-conformant" certification** for third-party packs and embedders;
  conformance suite as the compliance test, kernel licensing as the revenue.
- **The adversarial corpus as compounding data:** every red-team crack becomes a
  held-out test; years of that is a verification-hardness dataset no entrant
  shortcuts.
- **Signed evidence roots:** the stated honest ceiling ("a lying evidence root can
  still deceive") productized as a corpus-signing program. Recurring data-plane
  revenue.

### 3.6 Business model at full extension
Prosumer self-serve (JetBrains-style floor) + enterprise site licenses + OEM kernel
licensing (mark-preserving) + data plane (validity feeds, signed corpora) +
conformance certification. Certificate verification free forever.

## 4. Engineering direction (what "perfect" means here)

The engine's mission lock already states it: verify the whole checkable surface
hands-off, never fabricate a verdict, drive auto-resolution toward 0.95 with HARD
zero false-greens and zero false-accusations. The product translation:

1. **The kernel is the constitution.** Extraction per ADR-0014 behind the executable
   honesty contract; parity corpus as CI conformance gate in both repos; wire shape
   frozen. Nothing that can mint a green lives outside the kernel, ever.
2. **Every check is labeled DETERMINISTIC or ASSISTIVE at the surface** (already in
   the design handoff). Holding-match stays disclosed as LLM-assisted until a
   deterministic holding-support check exists. The determinism story never rests on
   a leg that does not bear weight.
3. **Greens are earned and shown.** Implement ADR-0013's safe-green set; the
   certificate clears what it can prove, refuses what it cannot, and scopes every
   refusal specifically (a refusal that names what was missing is a feature; a
   content-free refusal is a bug).
4. **A measured catch-accuracy number on the board.** Labeled set of hallucinated
   cites + bad contract claims; benchmark the deterministic checks; publish.
   "Sound by construction" loses to a number.
5. **Craft bar unchanged:** the design-handoff rebuild finishes (fonts → shell →
   lectern → verdict reveal → exhibit); the verdict reveal lands like a stamped
   judgment. The stillness is the drama.

## 6. The case against this plan (steelmanned, kept on file)

1. **The demand record is null and worsening.** 2/8 qualified, 0 costly-yes, G3=0.
   Every argument above is supply-side and structural. A universal certificate
   grammar with no first buyer is a slide. The council's "calls, not code" finding
   binds this plan too — which is why Phases 0–1 add almost no build beyond the
   already-ratified queue.
2. **Compulsion may simply never fire.** The doctrinally cheap path for courts is to
   keep loading duty onto the lawyer's Rule 11 signature forever; carriers may keep
   excluding rather than underwriting. Then Cachet is a good self-attestation tool
   business — real, profitable, not billions. The plan's answer: the option costs
   ~nothing, so buy it; but never spend Phase-3 money on Phase-1 evidence.
3. **The verbatim/semantic gap bounds the kernel.** "You can make a liability clause
   without the word liability" (call 02) is a real ceiling on contract-clause
   generality no framing dissolves. The kernel's honest domain is claims with
   verbatim-checkable anchors; the expansion ladder must respect it.
4. **The postural moves could metastasize.** Publishing schemas, drafting riders,
   courting carriers can become the comfort-zone substitute for validation calls.
   Each is capped (a week, a conversation, a draft) and none is allowed to displace
   an interview.

Resolution of the seats' one disagreement: the advocate says "fire the trigger"; the
historian says carriers are currently excluding, not underwriting. Synthesis adopted
above: pursue the carrier as **discovery now, pilot only on evidence**, and treat
the first coverage denial — not the next sanction — as the market's starting gun.
